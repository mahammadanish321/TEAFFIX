Simple Dependency

AI SERVICE
    │
    │ Detection Events
    ↓
BACKEND
    │
    ├── PostgreSQL/PostGIS
    ├── Vehicle Matching
    ├── Trajectory Engine
    └── Traffic Analytics
    │
    │ REST + WebSocket
    ↓
FRONTEND

Most important rule

AI owns AI APIs.
Backend owns business/data APIs.
Frontend owns UI only.

So your team can work independently without constantly modifying each other's repositories.
